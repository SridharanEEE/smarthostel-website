How does it work?

The proposed system combines occupancy detection and automatic appliance control.

1

Detect

A motion sensor checks for occupancy in the room.

2

Process

A microcontroller processes the sensor signal and applies the control logic.

3

Control

A suitable control module switches connected lights and fans according to the configured logic.
